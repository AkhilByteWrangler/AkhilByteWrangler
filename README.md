## Research Interests 

- **Large Language Models (LLMs)**: Exploring ways to make LLMs more effective, efficient, and adaptable while improving their ability to understand and generate human-like language.  
- **Responsible AI**: Developing frameworks and methodologies to ensure AI systems prioritize safety, fairness, and accountability in real-world applications.  
- **Explainable AI**: Enhancing transparency in AI decision-making to build trust and enable meaningful human-AI collaboration.  
- **Ethics in AI**: Investigating the implications of AI deployment and creating solutions that align with societal values and long-term sustainability.
